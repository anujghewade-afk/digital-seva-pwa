📖 Step-by-Step DigiLocker Guide

Step 1: Open DigiLocker

Open the official DigiLocker website or app.

Step 2: Login or Register

Enter your registered mobile number and follow the instructions to access your account.

Step 3: Find Your Documents

Find the documents available in your DigiLocker account.

Step 4: Access Your Document

Select a document to view its available details.

Step 5: Download or Share

Use the available options to download or share your document when required.

Step 6: Stay Safe

Never share your OTP, password or sensitive information with unknown people.
